Not a vending machine.

Your brand on the screen

Circuit's logo, colours and tone of voice on every screen, from choosing a scent to the thank-you.

Your next event after every sale

The moment a guest pays, the screen shows Circuit's next big night, like NYE, with a QR code to tickets. Circuit picks it, and it can change any night.

Pink trim on the unit itself

The front accents match Circuit's colours, so the unit looks like it was made for the room.

04 · Safety

Designed with the
door team in mind.

SPRAY is run by people who work venue doors, so the unit is built around the things that cause trouble on a busy night.

No glass in reach

Bottles are sealed behind the front panel. Guests only touch the screen, the card reader and the buttons.

No cash

Card-only means there's no float and nothing to break in for. It's never a target.

Fixed to the wall

A 30 kg steel body bolted to a solid wall, with a lockable chassis. It can't be knocked over or carried off.

In view of CCTV

We'll place it where cameras and staff already see it, out of the way of busy walkways and fire exits.

Nothing for staff to do

No stock behind the bar and no cash to reconcile. SPRAY handles any complaint, fault or refund directly.

Easy to switch off

Staff can turn it off at the socket in an emergency or if it looks faulty. We're on call for the rest.

SPRAY × CIRCUIT05 — GUEST SAFETY

Wrist and neck.
Never the face.

Sprays where it should

The five outlets sit flush in the panel below the display. Each one releases a single short, horizontal burst at wrist or neck height. Nothing sticks out to grab or point.

Instructions on the machine

Printed steps on the front panel: choose, pay, guide your hand or neck to the nozzle, press the button. No guesswork after midnight.

Warnings on every unit

A printed warning across the top of the panel: for external use only, avoid eyes and mouth, may contain allergens. One measured spray per payment, so nobody can keep spraying.

If something goes wrong

If spray gets in an eye, rinse it with clean water, the same first aid as any perfume. SPRAY handles every complaint directly and holds £2m public liability insurance for the units.

Real, lawful fragrances

SPRAY is responsible for every fragrance being a lawful cosmetic product and for the unit meeting electrical-safety rules.
